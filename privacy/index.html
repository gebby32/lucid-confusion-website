Privacy Policy for OmniStash

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

OmniStash is developed and operated by Lucid Confusion Creations, LLC (“Lucid Confusion Creations,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the OmniStash mobile application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

OmniStash is designed primarily as a local application.

Information you enter or capture in OmniStash may include inventory details such as item names, quantities, storage locations, dates, barcode information, activity history, and photos you choose to save.

This information is primarily stored on your device and is used to provide OmniStash’s inventory-management features.

OmniStash does not require you to create an OmniStash account.

2. Camera and Scanning Features

OmniStash may request access to your device camera so you can use features such as:

  • Barcode scanning
  • Date scanning
  • Item photography

Camera access is used only when needed for these app features. OmniStash does not use your camera in the background.

3. Product and Information Lookups

Some OmniStash features may use internet-based services to retrieve product or food information.

When you request an online lookup, information necessary to perform that lookup, such as a barcode, product name, or search query, may be transmitted to third-party data or search providers.

These services are used to return requested information to OmniStash. Lucid Confusion Creations does not use these lookup requests to create an advertising profile about you.

4. Purchases and OmniStash Pro

OmniStash offers an optional one-time purchase called OmniStash Pro through Google Play.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Lucid Confusion Creations does not receive or store your full payment-card information.

OmniStash uses RevenueCat to manage purchase status and determine whether OmniStash Pro has been unlocked.

RevenueCat may process information related to purchases and app usage necessary to provide this service, including an anonymous customer identifier, purchase history, purchase tokens, device or operating-system information, and related technical information.

RevenueCat’s own privacy practices are governed by its Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

OmniStash does not contain advertising.

OmniStash does not use advertising networks to track users for targeted advertising.

6. Analytics and Tracking

OmniStash does not create an OmniStash user account or require personally identifying profile information to use the application.

Third-party services required for purchases or online lookup functionality may receive limited technical information necessary to provide those services.

7. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be provided to third-party service providers only when necessary to operate features you choose to use, including:

  • Google Play for application distribution and purchase processing
  • RevenueCat for purchase validation and OmniStash Pro entitlement management
  • Online product, food, or search-data providers when you request a lookup

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, or safety of Lucid Confusion Creations, our users, or others.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Inventory information stored locally in OmniStash remains on your device until you delete it, reset applicable app data, or remove the application, subject to any backup or restore functionality provided by your device or operating system.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to those services’ respective policies and legal obligations.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your OmniStash purchase history or RevenueCat customer record, contact us at:

support@lucidconfusion.gg

We will process appropriate requests and, when applicable, request or perform deletion through the relevant service provider.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the information OmniStash collects and to use service providers appropriate to the functionality being provided.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

OmniStash is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected in a manner requiring deletion, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

11. Third-Party Services

OmniStash may interact with services operated by third parties. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Their privacy practices are not controlled by Lucid Confusion Creations.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as OmniStash changes or as legal, technical, or regulatory requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the revised version will include a new effective date and will be posted on the Lucid Confusion Creations website.
